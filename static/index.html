SMALL BY DESIGN

How this assistant works

01

Interpret

Identify the company, financial year and supported metric. Ambiguous or unsupported requests are declined.

02

Retrieve & calculate

Retrieve curated facts from official disclosures. Calculate margins, year-on-year growth and percentage-point changes in Python.

03

Show the evidence

Return the answer, table, formulas, source locators and accounting differences together.

This prototype uses deterministic intent rules, not an LLM or vector search. It combines a refreshable public-price adapter with curated filings. It has no document upload or conversational memory. A model could later propose a structured query, while this calculation and evidence layer remains responsible for financial outputs.

Historical annual sources do not record publication dates. Recent quarterly sources record announcement dates, not independently verified exact broadcast times. This prototype does not support point-in-time trading research.
